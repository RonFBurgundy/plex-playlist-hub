Drag this button up to your browser's Bookmarks Toolbar:

🎵 Send to Plexamp

Zero extensions or passwords. Safe, client-side browser helper.

How it works:
  1. Open open.spotify.com and view any playlist or your Liked Songs.
  2. Scroll down through your playlist so Spotify loads the songs in the page.
  3. Click 🎵 Send to Plexamp in your browser bookmarks bar.
  4. It securely bridges the songs to this Hub and opens the 1-click import confirmation!
